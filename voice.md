public facing pages: mobiwave.co.ke. 

MobiWave builds tools for real Kenyan businesses. It was started because bulk SMS in Kenya had too many middlemen, unreliable delivery, and no transparency.

The product is:
- an SMS platform that actually delivers (we route smartly across Safaricom, Airtel, Telkom)
- M-Pesa integration that doesn't need a PhD to set up
- USSD menus for organisations whose customers don't have smartphones
- WhatsApp broadcast when you need rich media plus delivery receipts

Our clients are mostly Nairobi-based or county-level: SACCOs, hospitals, logistics firms, government agencies, churches, and schools. The budget is usually KES 10k–100k/month. Decision makers want reliability, local support via WhatsApp, and a dashboard they can actually read.

No one cares about "cutting-edge AI-powered omnichannel transformation." They want to know: does it work on Safaricom? Does the MPesa integration reconcile properly? Can I see a delivery report? Who picks up the phone when something breaks?

The site needs to sound like people who actually run this stuff in Nairobi.
